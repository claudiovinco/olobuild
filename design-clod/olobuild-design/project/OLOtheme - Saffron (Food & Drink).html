Our story

A neighbourhood kitchen, run by the people who cook in it.

Saffron started as a six-table room with one wood oven. We still cook the way we did then — buying what's good that morning, writing the menu in the afternoon, and serving it the same night.

No deliveries we wouldn't eat ourselves, no dish that hasn't earned its place. Just a kitchen that takes its time.
