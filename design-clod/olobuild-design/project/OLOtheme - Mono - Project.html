Project 01 / Brand identity

Marisol — a coastline, in one mark.

Client
Marisol Hotels
Year
2026
Role
Identity, signage, web
Recognition
D&AD Wood Pencil

Marisol runs six small hotels along one stretch of Atlantic coast. They'd grown by acquisition, which meant six logos, six tones of voice and a website that felt like a committee. The brief was deceptively simple: make it one place.

One mark, all weather

We built the identity around a single horizon line that bends into an "M" — a mark that works cast in brass on a door, embroidered on a robe, or animated on a phone. Everything else got out of its way: one typeface, two inks, a lot of paper-coloured space.

Restraint as luxury

The hospitality category loves to shout. Marisol wanted the opposite — the confidence of a place that doesn't need to. So we treated silence as a material: wide margins, slow transitions, and a booking flow you can finish in four taps without ever seeing a carousel.

A year on, occupancy is up across all six properties and, more tellingly, guests have started getting the logo tattooed. We're choosing to read that as a good sign.
