Our story

From one vineyard to 38 countries

In 2019 our founders lost a night’s harvest to a pump that failed in silence. The off-the-shelf “smart farm” gear was either too fragile, too closed, or needed an engineer to install.

So they built their own: rugged, open, and simple enough that the person who works the field can install it. Today IOTfarm runs on a quarter of a million devices across vineyards, orchards, greenhouses and water utilities.

The road so far

How we got here

2019

The frozen pump

Founded after a lost harvest. First prototype node hand-built and buried in a Piedmont vineyard.

2021

Platform v1 & LoRaWAN

Launched the cloud platform and our own long-range gateways. First 1,000 devices online.

2023

Automations & open API

Valve control, webhooks and a public REST API. Crossed 50,000 active devices.

2026

A quarter-million nodes

250,000+ devices across 38 countries, 99.98% platform uptime, and a six-year battery in every node.
