About

Painting the edges of weather

Jonah Veld (b. 1986) works in oil and hand-ground pigment from a harbour studio. His large abstracts begin outdoors, in notebooks and weather, and are finished slowly indoors over months.

Exhibited across Europe and held in private and public collections, his work is represented by Atelier 9.

On the palette

Mix a colour

Painting is mostly mixing. Tap two or three pigments from my working palette and watch the colour settle — the same muddy, hopeful guesswork I do every morning.
