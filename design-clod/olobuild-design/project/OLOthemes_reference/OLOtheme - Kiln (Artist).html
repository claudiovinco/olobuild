From clay to keep

How a piece is born

I

Throw

Wet clay, the wheel, and a shape that finds itself.

II

Trim & dry

Footed, refined, then left to dry slowly for a week.

III

Glaze

Dipped in glazes mixed from raw oxides, by eye.

IV

Fire

Three days of wood flame. The kiln has the final say.
