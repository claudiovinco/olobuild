Follow the route

Carretera Austral · 1,240 km south
Day 1 · Km 0

Villa O'Higgins

The road begins exactly where the next one gives up.

End of the line, southbound. A single pump, a hand-painted sign, and 1,240 kilometres of gravel waiting to the north. We aired down the tyres and didn't speak for an hour.

Day 3 · Km 228

Caleta Tortel

A village with no streets — only cypress boardwalks.

You leave the truck at the top and walk in. Every house on stilts, every path made of planks, the whole place smelling of woodsmoke and wet timber.

Day 6 · Km 612

Río Tranquilo

Water so blue it looks edited.

We rowed out at first light to the marble caves — caverns the lake has been polishing for six thousand years. The guide cut the motor and let us just listen to it drip.

Day 9 · Km 920

Cerro Castillo

The castle that gives the range its name.

A full day on foot to the lagoon beneath the spires. Wind that knocks you sideways, then ten minutes of total stillness that made the whole trip worth it.

Day 11 · Km 1,240

Coyhaique

Tarmac again — and a strange reluctance to reach it.

Pavement, a proper coffee, phone signal. The road's over. We sat in the plaza too long, already plotting the way we'd come back and do it slower.
