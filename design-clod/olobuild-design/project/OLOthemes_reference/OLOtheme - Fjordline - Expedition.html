Norway · Lofoten

The Lofoten Traverse

8 days Moderate · hiking + kayak Max 12 travellers From €2,150 pp
The journey

Island to island, peak to sea

We start in the fishing village of Reine and work our way north — hiking the ridgelines by day, paddling the sheltered sounds, and sleeping in rorbu cabins and a wild camp or two.

It's a real traverse, not a basecamp loop. You'll feel the distance in your legs and earn every view. No technical climbing — just steady days on good trails with one of our Lofoten-born guides.
