Casa Navigli

TypeFull interior
Size110 m²
Year2025
PhotographyS. Conti

A 1930s apartment that had been "modernised" one too many times — our job was to give it back its bones, and a lot of its warmth.

We stripped away the laminate and dropped ceilings to find original parquet and generous proportions underneath. From there it was a careful rebuild: lime plaster walls, restored doors, and a palette pulled from the terracotta rooftops outside the window.

The furniture is a mix of vintage finds and a few custom pieces — a long oak table built for actual dinners, a reading chair positioned for the afternoon light. Nothing too precious; everything meant to be used.

They found the apartment our home was hiding inside it. We can't imagine living anywhere else now.
— Chiara & Luca, owners
