Helio —
solar, reframed.

ClientHelio Energy
ServicesBrand · Identity · Campaign
Year2025
RecognitionAwwwards SOTD

Helio came to us with great technology and a brand that looked like everyone else's in the category — blue gradients, stock skies, the lot.

We started by throwing out the clichés. Instead of selling panels, Helio would sell the feeling of independence — your own power, your own terms. That single idea reshaped the name, the identity and the entire launch.

The result is a warm, confident system built around a single graphic device — the rising mark — that works as a logo, a loading state, a billboard and everything in between.

Across out-of-home, social and a new site, the campaign lifted brand search by a third in its first quarter — and gave the team an identity they're genuinely proud to ship.
