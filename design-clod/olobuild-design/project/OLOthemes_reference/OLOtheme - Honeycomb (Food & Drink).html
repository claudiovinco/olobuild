Our story

One oven, one starter, a lot of mornings

Honeycomb began with a wild yeast starter named Doris and a market stall. Ten years on, Doris is still going — and still the boss.

We mill some of our own flour, prove everything slowly, and bake in small batches so it's always fresh. When it's gone, it's gone.
