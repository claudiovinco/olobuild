House on the Slope

A family house built into a north-Italian hillside — organised around a single concrete spine, opening south to the valley and the light.

The site fell away sharply to the south. Rather than fight the slope, we used it: a board-marked concrete spine steps down the hill, holding back the earth on one side and carrying the house on the other. Everything hangs off that one decisive line.
