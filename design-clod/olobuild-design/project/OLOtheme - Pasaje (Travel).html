The stay

A small hotel that
feels like a friend's house

Pasaje sits in a restored merchant's house off a quiet passage. Fourteen rooms, no two alike, all lime-washed walls, local linen and shutters that throw stripes of afternoon light.

Breakfast is whatever the market gave us, served in the courtyard. The roof is yours at sundown.

— Inés & the Pasaje team
