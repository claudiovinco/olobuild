About

I'm Nadia — a designer who'd rather remove ten things than add one.

For eight years I've worked between brand and digital, mostly with small teams who care about craft. Before going independent I led design at a studio in Copenhagen; now it's just me, which is exactly the right number of people.

I work in tight loops, show early, and treat your constraints as the brief — not the obstacle.
