The trip

Two countries, one great range

From the granite towers of Torres del Paine to the spires of Fitz Roy, this is Patagonia at its rawest — long days on foot, big weather, and the kind of silence you travel a long way to find.

We move slowly enough to actually be there: nights in mountain refugios, meals cooked over fire, and a guide who reads the sky better than any forecast.

The itinerary

Day 1–2

Arrive Punta Arenas → Puerto Natales

Meet the group, gear check, and the long beautiful drive north to the edge of the park.

2
Day 3–6

The "W" — Torres del Paine

Four days through the park's classic circuit: the towers at dawn, the French Valley, and Grey Glacier up close.

3
Day 7–9

Cross into Argentina → El Chaltén

Trekking capital of the south. Day hikes to Laguna de los Tres beneath Fitz Roy.

4
Day 10–11

Perito Moreno → home

Stand before the moving wall of ice, then the journey back with a group of new friends.
