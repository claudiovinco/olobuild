Questions, answered

Is there really a free plan?

Yes — Starter is free forever for up to 5 members, with 3 active workflows and 10 integrations. No card required.

Can I switch plans anytime?

Absolutely. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your billing settings — changes are prorated automatically.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

We offer 50% off Team plans for registered non-profits and open-source projects. Reach out and we'll set you up.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export everything at any time. After cancellation we keep your data for 30 days, then permanently delete it.
