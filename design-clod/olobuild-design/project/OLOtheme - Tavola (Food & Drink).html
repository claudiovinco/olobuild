Our story

One small kitchen,
three generations

Nonna Lucia opened Tavola with eight tables and one rule: cook for people the way you'd cook for family. Her ragù still simmers on the same stove.

Today her grandchildren run the pass — same recipes, same market at dawn, the same long Sunday lunches that go on until the coffee's cold.

— La famiglia Bertolini
