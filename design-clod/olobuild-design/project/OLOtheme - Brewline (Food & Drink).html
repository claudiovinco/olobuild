// brew it right

Pour-over in four moves

The ratio that works for most of our washed coffees. Adjust to taste — then write it on the bag.

1

Weigh

18g coffee, medium grind. 300g water at 94°C.

2

Bloom

Pour 50g, wait 40 seconds for the gas to escape.

3

Pour

Top up in slow circles to 300g over two minutes.

4

Enjoy

Drawdown by 3:00. Swirl, rest, taste, adjust.

We still roast every batch by ear and log every curve. When a lot's gone, it's gone — that's the point of buying it fresh.
